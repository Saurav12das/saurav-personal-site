YOUR READING, YOUR PACE

A feed is a quiet way
to keep up.

Add a feed link to your preferred RSS reader. New entries appear there as they’re published; your reader controls how often it checks and whether you get alerts.

  1. Choose a feed.Follow Everything, or choose individual topics above. You don’t need both.
  2. Add it to your reader.Copy the feed link into your RSS app’s “Add feed” or “Subscribe” field. Opening a feed directly may show XML; that’s normal.
  3. Read when you’re ready.Turn notifications on or off in your reader. Remove a feed there whenever you want to stop following.
