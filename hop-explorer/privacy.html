Privacy Policy

Hop Explorer

Effective October 21, 2026

Hop Explorer does not collect your journal. Your beers, photos, ratings, and notes are stored on your device. Device backups and links you choose to open are described below.

Data collection

Hop Explorer has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated backend. Your journal is not uploaded to us. Photo cutouts and flavor suggestions are processed on your device.

Data storage

All information you enter in the app — beer entries, tasting notes, ratings, flavor profiles, photos, and optional place names — is stored locally in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Depending on your settings, this data may be included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or computer backups), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a beer entry, the app copies your selected photo into its private on-device storage. Hop Explorer uses Apple's photo picker, which means the app can only ever access the specific photos you explicitly choose — it cannot browse or scan your photo library.

Places and external links

Hop Explorer does not request or read your device's location. You can type a city, taproom, or other place name into an entry. That name is saved with your journal. If you tap a place in Beer Map, Hop Explorer opens Apple Maps with that place name as a search query. Apple Maps handles that search under Apple's privacy policy; your beer entries, photos, ratings, and notes are not included.

Privacy and support links open this website in your browser. Your browser and the destination service handle those visits separately from the app.

Data deletion

You can delete individual entries in the app after confirming the deletion. Deleting the app removes its local journal. Any copies already included in device backups are managed through your iCloud or computer backup settings; deleting an entry does not erase older backups.

Children's privacy

Hop Explorer is an app about beer and is intended for users of legal drinking age. It is not directed at children and, as described above, collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, with the effective date revised above.
