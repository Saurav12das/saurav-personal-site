Hop Explorer

A personal beer journal for iPhone and iPad. Keep tasting notes, revisit favorite places, and watch your palate take shape.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your journal is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Hop Explorer has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated backend. Your journal may be included in your device backups. Opening a saved place in Apple Maps shares only that place name with Maps.

How do I back up my beer library?

Keep a current iCloud or computer backup of your device and check that Hop Explorer is included in it. Restoring a backup that contains the app's data restores the journal saved in that backup. The app does not provide its own cloud sync.

How do I delete a beer entry?

Open the beer from your Cellar, scroll to Delete Beer, and confirm. This removes the entry and its photos from the app. Removing a beer from Library only removes it from your favorites; it stays in your Cellar.

How do I erase all my data?

Deleting the app removes its local journal. Copies in earlier iCloud or computer backups are controlled by your backup settings and must be managed separately.

Why does the app ask for photo library access?

Only so you can attach photos of a beer to its journal entry. Photos you choose are copied into the app's private storage; the app never scans or uploads your library.

How do places and Beer Map work?

Type a city, taproom, or other place name when adding or editing an entry. Beer Map lists those places. Tap one to search for that place in Apple Maps. Hop Explorer does not request your device's location.

How do I edit a beer?

Open the beer from your Cellar and tap Edit. You can change its name, brewery, photo, style, rating, tasting notes, place, and flavor profile, then tap Save.

What iOS version do I need?

Hop Explorer requires iOS 17 or later.
