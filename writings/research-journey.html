QUESTION 01 / 05 2020

Better soil — compared with what?

The Soil Health Gap frames soil condition as a distance from an undisturbed reference within an agroecosystem. It gives improvement a benchmark, rather than treating an isolated test result as a verdict.

THE NEXT QUESTION

But soil and climate change the potential. Which sites can we fairly compare?

The reference and indicator matter; a gap is not a universal soil-health score.

Published paper

Soil Health Gap: A concept to establish a benchmark for soil health management

Maharjan, Das & Acharya · Global Ecology and Conservation · 2020

QUESTION 02 / 05 2022

Which soils belong in the same comparison?

The Cropland Reference Ecological Unit (CREU) groups land with comparable soil-forming and climatic conditions. It helps separate differences caused by management from differences a field inherits from its environment.

THE NEXT QUESTION

A comparable area is only the start. Where, within it, should the reference come from?

CREU supplies ecological context; it does not make every soil or management history identical.

Published paper

Cropland Reference Ecological Unit: A land classification unit for comparative soil studies

Das & Maharjan · Ecological Indicators · 2022

QUESTION 03 / 05 2024

What makes a reference defensible?

Reference-site selection uses ecological site descriptions and state-and-transition models to identify the appropriate reference plant community. The benchmark becomes an ecological condition to establish, rather than the nearest patch that looks undisturbed.

THE NEXT QUESTION

With a defensible reference in place, which measurements are useful enough to repeat?

Reference selection still requires field evidence and knowledge of disturbance history.

Published paper

Reference site selection based on state-and-transition models for soil health gap evaluation within cropland reference ecological units

Das, Hird, Maharjan, Stephenson & Kariyawasam · Soil Security · 2024

QUESTION 04 / 05 2026

Can measurement be consistent and interpretation local?

Tier 1 identifies a practical core: organic matter, pH, nitrate-N, and bulk density. Tier 2 interprets those measurements against ecologically matched reference distributions. The framework separates what to measure from what a result means in a particular soil and climate.

THE NEXT QUESTION

An ecological reference describes potential. What is a realistic next step for working cropland?

The reference dataset comes from Nebraska; broader transfer needs regional testing and calibration.

Published paper

A two-tier, reference-informed framework for soil health assessment: Minimum indicator sets and ecologically dynamic thresholds

Das, Gamage, Stephenson, Acharya, Daigh & Maharjan · Agrosystems, Geosciences & Environment · 2026

QUESTION 05 / 05 2026

How do we turn potential into attainable progress?

Hierarchical Land Classification connects ecological reference, recovery, soil-building, production, and degraded states. A synthesis of 1,059 soil organic carbon observations from 105 studies examines intermediate targets between today's condition and ecological potential.

THE NEXT QUESTION

The open question: do these context-aware targets predict useful outcomes across more farms and regions?

Preprint. Recovery and soil-building states were not statistically distinguishable; targets are neither universal nor equally spaced.

Preprint · not peer reviewed
