  1. Reading noteSeptember 2026Soil · Food · Community

    The Farm Is Here

    Jeff Tkach

    What do we lose when agriculture loses its community?

    Reading this book through the lens of long-term field research: soil, food, biodiversity, and the communities around a farm. My notes follow the evidence behind the story, and the questions still open, from the cost of transition to how we value healthy soil.

    A little context: I work at Rodale Institute. This is a personal reading, informed by that research perspective.

  2. Reading noteApril 10, 2025Food · Energy · Limits

    How to Feed the World

    Vaclav Smil

    What changes when a food-system solution has to work at scale?

    I expected a new paradigm; Smil brought me back to the constraints. Nutrients, energy, photosynthesis, and even the land needed to produce cover-crop seed complicate simple answers. Reading from within organic agriculture, I found myself asking what makes a practice scalable, and which costs we overlook when we promise more.
