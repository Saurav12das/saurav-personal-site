02 FOOD · ENERGY · LIMITS

READING NOTE · APRIL 10, 2025

How to Feed the World

Vaclav Smil

What changes when a food-system solution has to work at scale?

I expected a new paradigm; Smil brought me back to the constraints. Nutrients, energy, photosynthesis, and even the land needed to produce cover-crop seed complicate simple answers. Reading from within organic agriculture, I found myself asking what makes a practice scalable—and which costs we overlook when we promise more.
