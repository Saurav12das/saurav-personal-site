Bio

I'm curious about how the world works — from soil microbes to economic systems to AI. That curiosity is what drives me to build tools that tackle some of the biggest challenges in agriculture. Right now, that means Soil Health Exchange, OpenAgData, and TerraValue — making research accessible, soil intelligence actionable, and farm value visible.

If you're working on something similar, or just thinking about these problems — I'm always up for a conversation. Whether it's collaborating, brainstorming, or exploring ways to support this work, reach out at saurav12das@gmail.com.
