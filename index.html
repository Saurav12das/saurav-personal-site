01 — Thesis

Agriculture still lacks a coherent intelligence layer.

Why this · Why now

Research lives in PDFs. Soil decisions live in fragmented advice. Input buying happens through disconnected channels. A farm's ecological value is mostly invisible to markets. I'm building a connected stack to close those gaps — pointed at one problem from multiple entry points.

That direction comes directly from my background in soil microbiology and agricultural systems research. I care about making science legible, field decisions better, and agricultural infrastructure more useful for the people who actually work the land.

03 — Why me

Not a generic software founder hunting an ag market.

Founder portfolio

I came into this through soil systems, microbial ecology, carbon and nitrogen dynamics, and years of seeing how hard it is for real agricultural knowledge to move from papers and institutions into everyday decisions.

That's the founder portfolio beneath all of this: research depth, systems thinking, and product instinct pointed at the same problem. The ambition is not five unrelated things. It's one coherent intelligence layer for agriculture — from multiple entry points.
