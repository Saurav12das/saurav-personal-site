§ Research

The science depth beneath the build.

Open to collaboration

My founder work grows out of a longer research arc — environmental microbiology and arsenic biogeochemistry in Assam, soil health benchmarking and carbon-nitrogen dynamics at Nebraska, applied systems research at the Rodale Institute's Farming Systems Trial. That field experience is what makes the products real instead of speculative.

The dedicated Research Journey page traces the scientific questions, field contexts, and publications that shaped how I build — useful for funders, university collaborators, and anyone evaluating depth.

§ Background

Why I'm the one building this.

A bit of context

I'm not a generic software founder hunting an ag market. I came in through soil systems, microbial ecology, and years of seeing how hard it is for real agricultural knowledge to move from papers and institutions into everyday decisions. The ambition isn't five disconnected products — it's one coherent intelligence layer for agriculture, built from multiple entry points.

Below the line is the more personal layer of how I think — essays, side experiments, and a few things I love — for anyone curious about the philosophy beneath the work.
