Thesis

Agriculture still lacks a coherent intelligence layer. Research lives in PDFs, soil decisions live in fragmented advice, input buying still happens through disconnected channels, and a farm’s ecological value is mostly invisible to markets. I’m building a connected stack to close those gaps.

That direction comes directly from my background in soil microbiology and agricultural systems research. I care about making science legible, field decisions better, and agricultural infrastructure more useful for the people who actually work the land.

Ag Intelligence Stack

These products are connected by design, not by coincidence. Soil Health Exchange is the flagship and OpenAgData is the research substrate beneath it. The rest of the stack extends that same logic into transactions, valuation, and field-level workflow.

  • Flagship Soil Health Exchange — science-backed soil guidance that helps farmers ask better questions, get expert answers, and translate research into field decisions.
  • Research OpenAgData — geographic agricultural research intelligence that turns scattered studies into regional evidence people can actually search and use.
  • Market FarmDeck — a marketplace for farm inputs and equipment built on public trial data, designed to reduce phone-call agriculture and make comparison easier.
  • Value TerraValue — natural-capital valuation infrastructure for agricultural parcels, making soil, water, biodiversity, and resilience more legible.
  • Field FieldLog — an early field-management layer for capturing what is actually happening on the ground and feeding that intelligence back into the wider system.
