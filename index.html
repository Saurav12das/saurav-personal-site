07 · About

Why I build what I build.

I'm not a generic software founder hunting an ag market. I came in through soil systems, microbial ecology, and years of watching how hard it is for real agricultural knowledge to move from papers and institutions into everyday decisions.

Everything I build comes out of that gap. The essays are the philosophy underneath the work; the experiments are where I play; the watching and reading lists are proof I do occasionally sit down.

Funding, partnerships, and good conversations.

I'm actively raising and open to university collaborations and operator intros. Email is easiest — I read everything that comes in.
